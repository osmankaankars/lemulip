    Your Files Stay
    On Your Mac.

    Freewheel processes your files on your Mac. Built-in tools handle everyday work; additional formats can use optional local converters.

    The app has no account, subscription, or upload service.

    What Can I Try On This Page?

    A guided example of the file wheel, its modes, and the activity view. Every filename and result is a sample. To work with your own files, use the macOS app.

    Do I Need Extra Tools?

    Many image, PDF, audio, and archive operations use macOS or the system library. Some formats and media tools need an optional local engine. Freewheel shows which engine an operation needs.

    Where Should I Start?

    Read the Installation Guide, build Freewheel on your Mac, and try a copy of a simple image first. This is an early source preview; the project welcomes clear bug reports and contributions.
