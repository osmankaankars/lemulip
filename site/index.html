    Your Files Stay
    On Your Mac.

    Lemulip processes your files on your Mac. Built-in tools handle everyday work; additional formats can use optional local converters.

    The app has no account, subscription, or upload service.

    What Can I Try On This Page?

    A guided example of the file wheel, its modes, and the activity view. Every filename and result is a sample. To work with your own files, use the macOS app.

    Do I Need Extra Tools?

    The visual editors, TXT and basic RTF to Word export, and CSV/TSV to Excel export are built in. Basic RTF keeps text and paragraph formatting; RTF with images, tables, links, or other complex content uses LibreOffice. Each Excel export has one worksheet and keeps every value as text, including leading zeros and long numbers. Existing Office documents, slides, and some media formats use optional local converters. Check the format guide for each route.

    Check Supported Formats
    Where Should I Start?

    Download the universal DMG from Releases, open it, and drag Lemulip into Applications. It works on Apple silicon and Intel. Preview downloads are not yet notarized by Apple; the Installation Guide explains the first launch. You can also build from source.
